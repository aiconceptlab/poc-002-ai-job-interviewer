An AI interviewer that scores your answers. 🎙️

POC #002 — AI Job Interviewer

Choose a role. Answer a question out loud. Review the transcript.

Then see how your answer scores on:
→ Relevance
→ Structure
→ Evidence
→ Reflection

Each score comes with an explanation, a quote from your answer and a practical improvement. Refine your response, practise again and download the feedback.

Underneath: ElevenLabs for the voice interview + an LLM evaluator with a clear rubric and evidence checks.

Three roles. Nine questions. One small starter project you can make your own.

The source code is free on GitHub:
aiconceptlab / poc-002-ai-job-interviewer

The local sample needs no keys. Live voice and personalised scoring use vendor API credits. Carousel visuals and scores are illustrative; this is interview practice feedback.

Save this for your next build. Which role would you add first?
