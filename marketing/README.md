# Instagram launch pack · POC #002

Upload the five numbered PNGs in order. Each file is 1080 × 1440 pixels (3:4), with added margins around the complete design. This replaces the earlier exports. Select all five files from this pack and choose Portrait in the ratio screen shown in your screenshot. This edition uses plum, lilac and apricot. Copy caption.txt as the post text.

The images are concept illustrations, not screenshots or evidence of a live benchmark. The sample feedback totals 75/100: 4 + 4 + 4 + 3, multiplied by five. Generated with the built-in image tool; prompts and revision notes are in prompts.md.

## Alt text
1. **01-cover.png:** Dark plum cover reading "An AI interviewer that scores your answers." A lilac waveform sits beside an illustrative feedback card showing 75 out of 100.
2. **02-interview.png:** "Say it out loud. Then check the transcript." An interviewer asks about competing priorities; a candidate answer says they chose to fix onboarding first. Voice answers can be reviewed and edited; typing is also supported.
3. **03-scoring.png:** "A score should come with a reason." Sample scores are relevance 4/5, structure 4/5, evidence 4/5 and reflection 3/5. A supporting quote reads "I chose to fix onboarding first."
4. **04-improve.png:** "Make the next answer better." Feedback asks the user to explain how they would apply a lesson next time. A follow-up asks what they would do before the next planning meeting.
5. **05-build.png:** "Voice agent. LLM evaluator. One small build." A diagram connects ElevenLabs voice and transcript, an LLM with a rubric and evidence checks, and practical feedback. Source code and the local sample are free; live APIs consume credits.
